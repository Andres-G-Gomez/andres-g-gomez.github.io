I’m a machine learning engineer with a focus on computer vision and applying cutting-edge AI research to real-world problems. I earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Florida in 2024, where I concentrated on machine learning research in medical imaging and educational technologies.

Previously, I worked with [Dr. Catia Silva](https://faculty.eng.ufl.edu/catia-silva/) to develop a personalized, affect-aware multimodal educational assessment tool that integrated computer vision and time-series algorithms to interpret localized facial expressions, enabling more adaptable and expressive education-focused technologies than traditional emotion classification methods. Before that, I worked in [Dr. Wei Shao's](https://nephrology.medicine.ufl.edu/profile/shao-wei-1/) Artificial Intelligence in Medical Image Analysis Lab, where I engineered a novel deformable down-sampling module to enhance the adaptability of state-of-the-art vision models on MRI and CT medical image datasets.

Now, I’m focused on using AI for signal processing challenges at Blue Ridge Envisioneering, where I develop computer vision systems. Outside of work, I explore large language models in my side projects—building tools, creating educational notebooks, and experimenting with model fine-tuning.

You can browse my [projects page](https://andres-g-gomez.github.io/projects/) or connect with me on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/andres-gomez-7410a8160).
