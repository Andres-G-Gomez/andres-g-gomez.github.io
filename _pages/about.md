I'm a machine learning engineer with over four years of experience spanning ML research, applied engineering, and scientific computing, with a strong foundation in statistics and optimization. I earned my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Florida in 2024, and have worked across the full spectrum from academic research to building and shipping production AI systems.

Most recently, I co-founded a legal tech startup where I built and deployed two production agentic AI systems: an immigration intake assistant and a medical chronology tool. Both are fully deployed on GCP, orchestrating multi-step AI pipelines that use tools to retrieve and process information and persist structured outputs in PostgreSQL. I owned everything from model selection and prompt design to evaluation infrastructure and early compliance groundwork toward a HIPAA-ready architecture. Alongside this, I built and deployed a sponsor engagement web application for a Southeast US sustainability coalition that served over 200 attendees and was received with strongly positive feedback.

Before that, I spent just under a year and a half as an ML engineer at Parsons, working across signal processing and computer vision problems. I developed a novel algorithm for disentangling overlapping stochastic signals, which demonstrated state-of-the-art performance and was selected for production integration. On the computer vision side, I deployed models on neuromorphic hardware - work that required fully characterizing the devices, benchmarking performance, and implementing detection systems in the frequency domain.

My graduate research spanned medical imaging and educational technologies. Working with Dr. Catia Silva, I developed a personalized, affect-aware multimodal educational assessment tool that integrated computer vision and time-series algorithms to interpret localized facial expressions, enabling more adaptable and expressive educational technologies than traditional emotion classification methods - producing a [publication](https://andres-g-gomez.github.io/projects/1_project/) accepted into one of ASEE 2024's most competitive divisions. Before that, I worked in Dr. Wei Shao's AI in Medical Image Analysis Lab, where I contributed to research on multi-object segmentation of medical images, engineering a novel deformable downsampling module designed to improve the spatial adaptability of state-of-the-art vision models on the complex, heterogeneous structures present in MRI and CT datasets.
